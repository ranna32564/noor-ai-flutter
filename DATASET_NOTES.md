# Dataset provenance and review status

The Quran, dua, adhkar, title and guide JSON files were imported from the user-provided `NamazApp (1).zip` supplied in this conversation. This is provenance of the immediate input file, not proof of the original authorship or license.

Structural check:
- 114 surah objects
- 6,236 verse records
- 14 starter dua records
- 132 adhkar categories
- 4 introductory halal/haram categories

Not verified by this structural check:
- exact Uthmani orthography/diacritics against a trusted master text
- Bengali and English translation identity, accuracy, permissions or license
- complete hadith text, numbering, narrator and grading
- every dua's source and authenticity
- scholar review of rulings
- exhaustive coverage of all internet dua content

Before public distribution, record each dataset's upstream URL, author/translator, version/date, license/permission, checksum and reviewer. Verify Arabic and references independently. Never advertise this dataset as complete or certified until those checks are complete.
